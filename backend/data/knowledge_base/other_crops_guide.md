# Other Crops Guide: Groundnut, Sugarcane, Sweet Potato — Bangladesh
Source: compiled from BARI, BSRI and DAE production guides (public extension materials).

## Groundnut (Chinabadam) — season, soil, water
Grown in Rabi (sow November to mid-December) and Kharif-1 (February–March), harvested ~130 days later. Uniquely, groundnut prefers LIGHT sandy and char (riverbank) soils where most crops struggle — a good fit for sandy land. Low-to-medium water; needs moisture and calcium (gypsum) at the pegging and pod-fill stages.

## Groundnut — yield, fertilizer, economics
BARI Chinabadam-8, -9. Seed rate 40–48 kg/acre in-shell (seed is a notable cost). Yield 1.8–2.5 t/ha, about 19–27 maund/acre; planning figure 22. Low nitrogen (it fixes its own) plus TSP, MoP and — critically — gypsum split between sowing and pegging for pod fill. A strong option for sandy soils that suit few other cash crops.

## Groundnut — risks
Tikka leaf spot in humid weather; poor pod fill without gypsum/calcium; low risk overall on well-drained sandy land.

## Sugarcane (Aakh) — season, soil, water
A long-duration annual (~11 months). Plant setts in autumn (October–November) or spring (February–March); harvest the following winter. Best on deep loam and clay-loam; high total water need with many dry-season irrigations. Ties up the land for nearly a year, so the profit-per-month is modest.

## Sugarcane — yield, fertilizer, economics
BSRI Ganda varieties. Yield 40–50 t/ha, about 430–540 maund/acre; planning figure 450. Split urea across the long season plus full TSP, MoP, gypsum, zinc; earth up and prop against lodging. Returns depend heavily on mill payment timeliness or gur (jaggery) prices.

## Sugarcane — risks
Long land occupation (opportunity cost), stem and top borer, and delayed mill payments. Only suitable where the farmer can wait ~11 months for return.

## Sweet Potato (Mishti Alu) — season, soil, water
Rabi crop planted from vine cuttings mid-October to mid-December; harvest ~120 days. Like groundnut, it thrives on light sandy and char soils and is drought-tolerant with minimal irrigation — a cheap, hardy option for poor sandy land and tight budgets.

## Sweet Potato — yield, fertilizer, economics
BARI Mishti Alu varieties. Yield 10–15 t/ha, about 110–160 maund/acre; planning figure 130. Modest fertilizer: split urea + TSP, MoP. Lift vines during weeding to stop rooting at the nodes so tubers size up.

## Sweet Potato — risks
Sweet potato weevil in dry cracked soil — keep ridges earthed up and avoid soil cracking. Otherwise one of the lowest-risk, lowest-cost crops available.
