# Aus Rice Production Guide — Bangladesh
Source: compiled from BRRI "Adhunik Dhaner Chash", DAE crop calendar (public extension materials).

## Season and sowing window
Aus is the pre-monsoon (Kharif-1) rice crop, the third rice season after Aman and Boro. Direct-seed mid-March to end-April on early showers; harvest July–August. Duration is short, about 95–105 days. Aus frees the field for T. Aman, giving an Aus–Aman–fallow or Aus–Aman–Boro pattern.

## Soil and water
Best on clay-loam and loam medium land that holds early rain. Aus is mostly rainfed and depends on timely pre-monsoon showers for establishment; a dry March–April spell is the main risk. Once the monsoon sets in, water is rarely limiting, but flash floods at harvest can damage the crop. Not suited to droughty sandy soil without irrigation.

## Varieties, seed and management
Modern direct-seeded varieties: BRRI dhan48, BRRI dhan98 (short duration, ~100 days). Seed rate 12–15 kg/acre for broadcast or line sowing. Split urea in three: at tillering (25 DAS), and panicle initiation (45 DAS), with basal TSP/MoP/gypsum.

## Yield and economics
Aus yields less than Aman or Boro: about 3.0–3.5 t/ha, roughly 32–38 maund/acre; planning figure 35. Input cost is lower than Boro (rainfed, short), making it a modest but low-risk monsoon-start crop.

## Major risks
Drought at establishment (March–April), flash flood at harvest, stem borer and rice bug. Choose short-duration varieties to escape late-season flooding.
