# Potato Production Guide — Bangladesh
Source: compiled from BARI potato production guide, DAE crop calendar, BARC FRG-2018 (public extension materials).

## Season and planting window
Potato is a Rabi crop. Plant mid-November to mid-December when night temperature falls below 20°C. Planting before 10 November risks heat rot of seed tubers; after 20 December the crop meets hot March air during bulking and yields fall. Duration 85–95 days; harvest February to early March.

## Soil suitability
Loose, well-drained loam and sandy-loam are ideal — tubers need easy expansion. Sandy soil works with more irrigation and fertilizer splitting. Heavy clay produces deformed tubers and rot; avoid fields that waterlog after irrigation.

## Recommended varieties
Diamant and Asterix (processing + table), Cardinal (early market), BARI Alu-7/25/41. Certified seed tubers matter more than variety — degenerated home-saved seed can halve yield. Seed rate: about 600 kg (16 maund) of seed tubers per acre.

## Water management
4–6 light irrigations: at stolon initiation (20 DAS), tuberization (30–35 DAS), and during bulking every 12–15 days. Never flood above the ridge top. Stop irrigation 10 days before harvest to harden skins.

## Typical yield
With certified seed and balanced fertilizer: 20–25 t/ha, i.e. about 190–240 maund per acre. Planning figure: 200 maund/acre on loam with certified seed.

## Storage and market
Harvest-time (Feb-Mar) prices are the season's lowest; cold-storage until June–September typically adds 40–80% to price but costs about 350–450 BDT per maund including handling. Price crash years happen when national acreage spikes — check storage availability before planting large area.

## Major risks
Late blight (Phytophthora) in foggy 10–20°C weather can destroy a field in a week — prophylactic mancozeb sprays from 35 DAS during fog spells, switch to cymoxanil+mancozeb at first lesion. Cutworm at establishment. Price volatility is the biggest financial risk: budget for cold storage or contract sales.
