# Soil and Water Suitability Reference — Bangladesh Field Crops
Source: compiled from SRDI land/soil resource guides, BARC FRG-2018 and FAO crop water references (public materials).

## Soil texture quick reference
Clay / clay-loam: holds standing water; best for transplanted rice (Aman, Boro); poor for potato and monsoon maize (waterlogging, deformed tubers). Loam: the all-rounder — good for every crop; best for maize, potato, wheat, mustard, jute. Sandy / sandy-loam: drains fast, leaks nitrogen and water; workable for potato and maize with extra irrigation splits; poor for rice (percolation) unless a plough-pan exists. Silt-loam (floodplain): good for jute, wheat, mustard; fine for rice on medium land.

## Water availability classes
Rainfed only: T. Aman rice (monsoon), Kharif maize, jute and mustard-on-residual-moisture fit; Boro rice and potato do NOT fit. Tubewell/canal (reliable): all crops possible including Boro. Limited irrigation (1–3 waterings): wheat, mustard, Kharif maize; avoid Boro.

## Crop water requirement (total, indicative)
Boro rice 1000–1200 mm (nearly all irrigation) — highest. T. Aman rice 800–1000 mm (mostly monsoon rain). Kharif maize 400–500 mm (mostly rain). Potato 300–400 mm (4–6 light irrigations). Wheat 250–300 mm (2–3 irrigations). Mustard 150–250 mm (0–2 irrigations) — lowest.

## Monthly rainfall pattern (indicative, NW Bangladesh e.g. Rangpur)
Jan 8 mm, Feb 15 mm, Mar 25 mm, Apr 75 mm, May 220 mm, Jun 350 mm, Jul 400 mm, Aug 320 mm, Sep 250 mm, Oct 120 mm, Nov 10 mm, Dec 6 mm. Monsoon (Jun–Sep) supplies ~80% of annual rain. Rabi crops (Nov–Mar) depend almost entirely on irrigation.

## Season definitions
Kharif-1: mid-March to June (pre-monsoon; jute, aus rice, summer maize). Kharif-2 / Aman season: July to October (monsoon; T. Aman rice, Kharif maize). Rabi: November to February (dry winter; Boro rice, wheat, potato, mustard). In July–August, the practical planting choices are T. Aman rice and late Kharif maize; Rabi crops are planned for after mid-October.

## Matching budget to crop (per-acre working capital, indicative)
Low budget (< 20,000 BDT/acre): mustard, jute, T. Aman on own labour. Medium (20,000–35,000): T. Aman, wheat, Kharif maize, Boro. High (> 45,000): potato (seed tubers alone ~18,000 BDT/acre). If the farmer's budget is below the crop's cash cost, recommend the cheaper crop or a smaller planted area rather than under-fertilizing.
