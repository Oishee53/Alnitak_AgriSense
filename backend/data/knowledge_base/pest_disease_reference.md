# Common Pest and Disease Reference — Bangladesh Field Crops
Source: compiled from DAE IPM leaflets, BRRI and BARI plant protection guides (public extension materials).

## Rice — stem borer
Symptom: dead-hearts at tillering, white-heads at heading. Peak in Aman season August–October. Threshold: 10% dead-hearts. Prevention: clip seedling tips before transplanting, install bird perches, light traps. Treatment: carbofuran or chlorantraniliprole granules; cost roughly 600–900 BDT/acre per application.

## Rice — brown planthopper (BPH)
Hopper-burn patches from field centre, late season, favoured by dense canopy + excess urea + standing water. Scout base of hills weekly after 45 DAT. Treatment: drain field, spray pymetrozine; avoid broad-spectrum sprays that kill spiders (BPH's natural enemies).

## Rice — blast and bacterial leaf blight (BLB)
Blast: diamond lesions, favoured by cool nights + humid days (Boro Feb-Mar); BRRI dhan28 highly susceptible; spray tricyclazole at first sign. BLB: yellow wavy leaf edges after storms in Aman; no chemical cure — manage by avoiding excess urea and draining.

## Maize — fall armyworm (FAW)
Ragged whorl feeding with sawdust-like frass. Scout weekly 20–60 DAS. Threshold 10% infested plants. Hand-crush egg masses; spray spinetoram or emamectin benzoate into whorls in evening; rotate chemistry. Cost ~700–1000 BDT/acre per spray.

## Potato — late blight
Water-soaked leaf lesions with white mould under-surface, in foggy 10–20°C spells (Dec–Jan). Can destroy a crop in 5–7 days. Prevent: prophylactic mancozeb every 7–10 days during fog; at first lesion switch to cymoxanil+mancozeb or dimethomorph. Budget 2–4 sprays, ~800 BDT/acre each.

## Mustard — aphid
Colonies on shoots and pods in cool cloudy weather. Threshold ~50 aphids/plant on 10% plants. Ladybird beetles usually control early colonies — spray malathion only past threshold, in late afternoon to spare pollinators (mustard needs bees).

## Jute — hairy caterpillar
Gregarious larvae skeletonize leaves. Collect and destroy egg-mass leaves at 30–50 DAS; spray only on outbreak.

## Wheat — blast
Bleached spikes above the flag leaf in warm (>22°C) humid February. No rescue once spikes bleach. Prevention: sow by early December, use BARI Gom-33, one propiconazole spray at heading in risk years.

## General weather-pest rules
Prolonged cloudy humid spells: expect aphid (mustard) and blight (potato) pressure. Post-storm Aman fields: expect BLB. Dense over-fertilized canopies: expect BPH and lodging. After any >20 mm rain during grain drying: turn and re-dry grain to prevent mould.
