# Boro Rice Production Guide — Bangladesh
Source: compiled from BRRI "Adhunik Dhaner Chash", BARC Fertilizer Recommendation Guide 2018, DAE crop calendar (public extension materials).

## Season and sowing window
Boro is the fully irrigated dry-season (Rabi) rice crop. Seedbed sowing: mid-November to mid-December. Transplanting: early January to mid-February. Harvest: mid-April to late May. Boro gives the highest rice yields in Bangladesh but has the highest water and input cost.

## Soil suitability
Clay and clay-loam soils are best (hold irrigation water). Loam is workable with more frequent irrigation. Sandy soil is unsuitable — percolation losses make irrigation cost prohibitive.

## Recommended varieties
BRRI dhan28 (short duration, widely grown but blast-susceptible), BRRI dhan29 (long duration, highest yield), BRRI dhan89 and BRRI dhan92 (modern high-yield replacements for dhan29), BRRI dhan74 (zinc-enriched). Hybrid varieties yield 10–15% more with higher seed cost.

## Seedling management
Use 35–40 day old seedlings in January transplanting (older seedlings tolerate cold). Protect seedbeds from cold with polythene cover on nights below 15°C. Spacing 20 × 15 cm, 2–3 seedlings per hill.

## Water management
Boro needs roughly 1000–1200 mm of water in total, nearly all from irrigation (dry-season rainfall December–March is only 5–40 mm/month). Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) with a perforated pipe saves 20–25% irrigation water: irrigate when water level falls 15 cm below soil surface, except keep continuous standing water from booting to grain filling. Water stress at panicle initiation or flowering causes severe sterility.

## Typical yield
Modern inbred varieties: 5.5–6.5 t/ha (about 55–65 maund/acre); hybrids up to 7.5 t/ha. A realistic planning figure is 60 maund/acre with full irrigation and balanced fertilizer.

## Major risks
Cold injury to seedlings in January; irrigation failure or diesel/electricity cost spikes; blast disease on dhan28 in humid February-March; brown planthopper late season; pre-monsoon hail and nor'wester storms at harvest (April-May) — harvest promptly at 80% ripening.
