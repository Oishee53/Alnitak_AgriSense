# Fertilizer Recommendation Guide (Field Crops) — Bangladesh
Source: compiled from BARC Fertilizer Recommendation Guide 2018 (FRG-2018) and DAE leaflets (public extension materials). Doses are per acre for medium-fertility soil; reduce ~20% where 2 t/acre cowdung is applied.

## T. Aman rice fertilizer dose
Urea 60–70 kg/acre, TSP 20–25 kg/acre, MoP 25–30 kg/acre, gypsum 15 kg/acre. Apply full TSP, MoP, gypsum at final land preparation. Split urea in 3 equal top-dressings: 15 DAT (after establishment), 30–35 DAT (active tillering), 50–55 DAT (panicle initiation). Do not top-dress urea right before forecast heavy rain — runoff loses 30–40% of the nitrogen; wait until the field only has thin standing water.

## Boro rice fertilizer dose
Urea 100–110 kg/acre, TSP 35–40 kg/acre, MoP 40–45 kg/acre, gypsum 25 kg/acre, zinc sulphate 4 kg/acre. Basal: all except urea. Urea in 3 splits at 20, 40 and 60–65 DAT. On sandy patches split urea 4 times — light soils leak nitrogen.

## Maize fertilizer dose
Urea 100–110 kg/acre, TSP 50 kg/acre, MoP 40 kg/acre, gypsum 20 kg/acre, zinc sulphate 4 kg/acre, boric acid 2 kg/acre. Basal: 1/3 urea + all others. Top-dress 1/3 urea at knee-high (V8, ~25 DAS) with earthing-up, 1/3 before tasseling (~45 DAS). Always earth up after top-dressing to reduce lodging.

## Potato fertilizer dose
Urea 90–100 kg/acre, TSP 60 kg/acre, MoP 100–110 kg/acre (potato is potash-hungry), gypsum 40 kg/acre, zinc sulphate 4 kg/acre, boric acid 3 kg/acre, cowdung 4 t/acre. Basal: 1/2 urea + everything else in the ridge. Remaining 1/2 urea at 30–35 DAS with final earthing-up. Excess urea delays tuberization and softens skin.

## Wheat fertilizer dose
Urea 70–75 kg/acre, TSP 35 kg/acre, MoP 20 kg/acre, gypsum 25 kg/acre, boric acid 2.5 kg/acre. Basal: 2/3 urea + all others at sowing. Remaining 1/3 urea immediately after the crown-root-initiation irrigation (20–25 DAS) — top-dressing on dry soil wastes nitrogen.

## Mustard fertilizer dose
Urea 50–55 kg/acre, TSP 35 kg/acre, MoP 15 kg/acre, gypsum 30 kg/acre (sulphur boosts oil %), boric acid 2 kg/acre, zinc 2 kg/acre. Basal: 1/2 urea + all others. Remaining 1/2 urea at 18–22 DAS before flowering, ideally with a light irrigation.

## Jute fertilizer dose
Urea 40–45 kg/acre, TSP 15 kg/acre, MoP 15 kg/acre, gypsum 18 kg/acre. Basal: 1/2 urea + others at final prep. Top-dress 1/2 urea at 30 DAS after thinning-weeding.

## Soil-type adjustments
Sandy soils: split nitrogen into more, smaller doses (leaching loss) and add 25% extra MoP. Clay soils: full basal doses are safe; drainage matters more than dose. Saline coastal soils: use gypsum, avoid MoP over-dose. Where cowdung/compost 2 t/acre is used, cut urea and TSP by about 20%.

## Weather rules for fertilizer timing
1. Never top-dress urea within 48 hours before forecast heavy rain (>20 mm) — nitrogen washes away.
2. Do not apply urea on drought-stressed dry soil — irrigate first, then apply.
3. Apply gypsum and zinc only as basal; they do not work as top-dressing.
