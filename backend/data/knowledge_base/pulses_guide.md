# Pulses Production Guide: Lentil, Chickpea, Mungbean — Bangladesh
Source: compiled from BARI pulse crop guides and DAE crop calendars (public extension materials). Pulses are the lowest-cash-cost Rabi options and fix nitrogen, improving the soil for the next crop.

## Lentil (Masur) — season, soil, water
Rabi crop. Sow late October to mid-November on residual moisture after Aman; harvest February. Duration ~110 days. Best on loam and silt-loam medium-high land; tolerates lighter soil. Very low water need — usually zero to one irrigation. Lowest cash cost of the Rabi field crops, which makes it a strong recommendation for tight budgets.

## Lentil — varieties, yield, fertilizer
BARI Masur-6, -7, -8 (Stemphylium-blight tolerant). Seed rate ~15 kg/acre. Yield 1.2–1.5 t/ha, about 13–16 maund/acre; planning figure 14. Fertilizer is light: a small starter dose of urea plus TSP, MoP, gypsum and boron at sowing — pulses need little nitrogen because they fix their own.

## Lentil — risks
Stemphylium blight in foggy cloudy spells (spray mancozeb), aphids at podding, and waterlogging from unseasonal rain.

## Chickpea (Chola/Gram) — season, soil, water
Rabi crop. Sow late October to late November; harvest February–March. Duration ~120 days. Deep-rooted and drought-tolerant; grows on residual moisture with little or no irrigation. Prefers loam and silt; do not over-pulverize the seedbed. Good on medium-high land that drains well.

## Chickpea — varieties, yield, fertilizer
BARI Chola-5, -9, -10. Seed rate 12–14 kg/acre. Yield 1.2–1.5 t/ha, about 14 maund/acre. Light urea + full TSP, MoP, gypsum and boron at sowing.

## Chickpea — risks
Pod borer (Helicoverpa) at flowering/podding — use pheromone traps and spray only past threshold; botrytis grey mould and wilt in wet years.

## Mungbean (Mug) — season, soil, water
Very short-duration pulse (~65 days) grown in Kharif-1 (sow mid-February to end-March) and after Aman. Its speed lets it fill a fallow gap between two main crops. Loam and sandy-loam best; low water need with 1–2 irrigations in dry spells.

## Mungbean — varieties, yield, fertilizer
BARI Mug-6, -8. Seed rate 5–6 kg/acre. Yield 1.0–1.3 t/ha, about 11–14 maund/acre; planning figure 12. Light fertilizer as for other pulses. Mungbean commands a high market price and improves soil nitrogen for the following crop.

## Mungbean — risks
Yellow mosaic virus (spread by whitefly) and thrips; the short season limits total exposure. Harvest in 1–2 picks as pods blacken.
